Freighters

Capital ships of your own: how to get one, what is aboard, how to call and move it, crew and boundaries.

A freighter is a capital ship 150 to 950 blocks long that carries your base through space: a hangar for your ships, a hold of storage cells, a hall to build in, crew rooms and a bridge. It stays in open space — the Solar System, the Interrift, the Depths, the far roads — and never lands in a world. One freighter per player.

Every hull is put together from its own bow, middle, stern, citadel and mast and painted in one of six faction palettes: the bridge window and the station market show the parts by name.

How to get one

  • A distress call. Fly an armed ship in open space, away from stations, for about ten minutes: the instruments catch a call. Come closer than 220 blocks and shoot down the Drifters — those at the breaches first; the bar at the top is the freighter's hull. Then answer the captain (;) and take the ship. The first call is always class C; later ones come after an hour of flight and may be of any class. Everyone who dealt 20 damage gets an offer of their own, and it waits three game days.

  • A station. The order board of every people's station has “Freighters & Frigates”: three ships a day, with a drawing you can turn.

    Trust makes it cheaper. Payment comes from your inventory and from the hold of the ship on the pad.

  • An exchange. A freighter you already own counts for half the price of its class. The base stays exactly where it stands, the hold and the ships move over, the key is the same. If something does not fit, the list says what and how much — and nothing is taken.

A lost key is replaced for 4 of the station's supply at any board that sells freighters.

Calling, docking, jumping

  • In open space use the Freighter Key, or the “Call the Freighter” key (unbound by default; the key item may lie anywhere in the inventory), or the button in your ship's console: the freighter jumps in with its hangar toward you. It is free.
  • Fly into the glowing hangar mouth on the port side and you step out on the deck. You may also fly in on foot in a spacesuit.
  • Leave by ship through the hangar console (sneak-click picks the next ship), on foot through the airlock, in a spacesuit.
  • The command console on the bridge moves the freighter itself — to a body of the system or to your ship.

Aboard

The floor lines lead the way: amber to the hangar, white to the tower. Stand in an antigrav shaft and hold jump or sneak to ride up and down; the chart table on the command deck opens the map of the decks. There is air and Earth's gravity. Walls cannot be broken, you build in the hall, and machines work there as anywhere while somebody is on the decks. The bunks set no spawn point.

The boundary. An invisible boundary stands behind the walls and windows: creative flight, elytra, pearls, chorus fruit, boats, explosions, broken windows and other mods' teleports stay inside. The ways out are the hangar, the pilot seat, the hatch and the airlock (an operator's /tp works). Ship decks and raid decks have the same boundary.

Friends. The bridge console keeps the freighter's own list: friends dock, walk and build; calling and jumping is for the owner, and a ship leaves the hangar only with someone who may fly it.

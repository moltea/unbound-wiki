Peoples’ stations, orders and trust

The order board, the ring race, convoys, trust standings and the peoples’ great gifts.

At the dock of every people’s station stands an order board Order Board. Walk up and right-click it: the board holds one order of each kind per day, the same for every player, while what you take is yours alone. You can hold three orders at once. The reward is your road’s supply (stardust, void shards, deep embers), emeralds and the people’s trust. The mouse wheel flips through the cards.

The orders you have taken are always at hand in the task window (the F7 key), on the Orders tab: what to do, which station gave it, where to fly and how many blocks away it is, how long is left and what you will get; for “bring” orders, how many you already carry. Orders are taken, dropped and handed in at the board.

Ring race

Take the race and fly to the station: a track of twelve rings of light rises around it. The clock starts at the first ring. The next ring shines brighter than the rest and points the way on with a beam. Missing a ring does not end the race but adds 5 seconds. Your instruments show the timer, the ring number and how far behind the station record you are. The record (name and time) is shown on the race card at the board. A freighter makes it on the limit, a scout with time to spare.

At Interrift stations the rings are woven of threads, at Depths stations they are made of magma. The first race you hand in gives the Racer's Ribbon: a pennant of light flies behind your ship, a golden one for a record holder.

Convoy

Take a convoy and fly your ship to the meeting point (a mark in the sky, 350 blocks from the station): the station’s cargo drone comes out to meet you. The drone only moves while you are within 160 blocks and heads for a buoy 3,000–3,600 blocks away. At one third and two thirds of the way drifters attack it, three ships first, then four. The raiders go for the cargo and ignore your ship until you shoot at them, but every second ship of a wave is a hunter that comes straight for you. Choose whom to shoot first: while you chase the hunter, the others wreck the drone (its hull is 120; a wave nobody drives off destroys it in a little over a minute). Slow down as you close in: at full speed it is easy to miss the ships circling the drone. On a class B Hornet with a single Pulse Cannon II the convoy is risky — you reach the buoy with about half your hull; on a class A ship with Cannon III and a shield it is easy. Fall behind for more than a minute and the drone turns home, and the convoy fails. At the buoy the drone jumps, and you can hand the order in at the board. If you own a station, its cargo drones on their runs also go under the people’s guard. You can only escort one convoy at a time.

Trust grows not only with the station peoples: a people’s task in their settlement gives +18, a tablet shown to the elder +9, defeating the master of a space +60 to every people of that space. Striking one of the people costs −30.

Great gifts

To a Sworn friend the station board first shows a gift card with an “Accept” button. A gift cannot be bought, and it does one clear thing. The gift stays with you forever, even if you lose the item itself. While the item is in the pilot’s inventory, the people’s banner flies behind the ship.

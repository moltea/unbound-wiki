Nether and End changes

Stages of the End and the Nether: Sewn Seams and Split Seams, vent mouths, new weather and events, the creatures of the End and the Nether themselves, and the server owner's switch.

The Nether and the End are no longer just platforms for instruments and transport: they change as you play. An opened rift or vent, a slain guardian and a flight beyond it raise the world's stage — the seams on the End islands come apart, the vent mouths of the Nether heat up, and their own weather, events and creatures arrive. The instrument sky and the journal are personal, but the changes themselves are shared by the whole server.

Stages

A stage belongs to the End and to the Nether themselves, not to a player: the first one to open what is needed raises it, and everyone sees the changes. A stage only goes up.

  • When a stage rises, everyone in that world and the one who opened it get a line: who opened it and what the world is called now.
  • Old worlds catch up: when a player enters the End or the Nether, the world learns what they have opened before (rifts, vents, flights, the dragon, Withers killed) — and the stage takes its place.
  • The most visible changes follow not the whole world but the place: in the End, the side of the sky of an open rift; in the Nether, the biomes of an open vent (below).

The server owner's switch

The changes can be switched off: the game rule Nether and End changes (in the Game Rules screen or with /gamerule; on 26.3 it is <mod id>:nether_end_changes, on 1.21.1 and 1.20.1 <mod id>.nether_end_changes) or the line "nether_end_changes": false in config/<mod id>-server.json (read when the server starts). The changes run only when both are on.

Switched off, stages and opened places still build up, but the world does not show them: seams fall asleep, vent mouths cool down, stage weather and events do not come, new creatures do not come out, the Void Shoals and the Giant digs stand still. Switch it back on and everything returns within a second. An operator reads and sets the stage with the mod's command stage end|nether [get|set N].

Vent mouths

Vent mouths stand across the Nether — cold cones, cracks and pipes of Mouth Crust with a Mouth Throat in the middle; while the Nether sleeps, the throat now and then thumps under the ground (heard from afar).

  • Stage 1 — the crust cracks into a net of glowing cracks, the mouth breathes heat and breathes out a Deep Ember (no more than one lying nearby).
  • Heat charging: a Coal Generator Mk1 without fuel beside a hot throat, above it or two blocks above burns on the vent's breath; the throat then cools by a step and heats up again within a few minutes.
  • Stage 2 and the family's vent open — the mouth burns bright and grows the matter of its world around it (no more than six growths): for the Forge Gilded Slag, for the Ash Soul Cinder and bone blocks, for the Mycelium Crimson Growth, Warped Growth and Glowing Thread, for the Lament Tear Vein and crying obsidian. So the matter of the fire worlds can be had without the bell.
  • Journal stage Vent Mouth — stand on the crust of a mouth.

Events

  • Vent Breach (from stage 1): 40–90 blocks from a player, on wild ground (not in a fortress or a bastion), a new mouth breaks through the ground — a pillar of heat, a ring of cracks, a tremor — and stays. So mouths come to old worlds too. Journal stage Vent Breach — be near when it happens.
Spoiler: this describes a place reached late in the game.
  • The Vault Trembles (from stage 3): a double blow every eight seconds; a second before each, dust falls from the vault — step out from under it; on the blow, basalt boulders break loose, and at the end comes a collapse: a mound of deep basalt with nests of deep embers. Journal stage Vault Collapse — be near it.
  • From stage 3, glowing funnels turn on the lava seas where the Depths show through the Nether.

The light of the Nether lives too: the fog and the glow of the lava on the vault slowly warm and cool, like breathing. All of it is done without shaders, and with a shader pack only the fog color changes.

The End

The End changes by the sides of the sky: each outer island belongs to the rift closest by compass direction as seen from the middle of the End (Facet — southwest, Hive — north, Breath — east, Eye — southeast). Closer than 256 blocks to the middle (the main island, the pillars, the portals) nothing changes.

Sewn Seams and Split Seams

Sewn Seams lie on the outer islands — narrow cracks in the end stone that barely smolder, stitched across with thick dark thread (Seam Stitch); in the middle is a Seam Sign with the Ringwright sign "up", and a knock sounds in the silence. Use the sign with an empty hand — "look up"; the sign lights up when the rift of its side is open. Journal stage The Sewn Seam.

  • Stage 1 — open a rift in the riftscope, and the seams on its side come apart into glowing Split Seams (Split Seam): a veil in the color of the world sways over the crack, glowing cracks run over the ground. Your rifts now burn on the ordinary sky of the End as well.
  • Stage 2 — a split seam takes the look of its world: prism crystals of the Facet (Prism Druse), dripping wax of the Hive (Wax Comb), the burning violet vapor of the Breath (burns whoever stands in it without a helmet), the blinking Eye (teleports endermen nearby). On the horizon, on the side of each open rift, its color smolders.
  • A split seam broken with a pickaxe gives a pinch: 1–2 Void Shard and the matter of its world. The seam comes apart again at once — angrier: for five minutes it burns whoever stands on it and gives no pinch. Pistons and explosions do not move a split seam, and it cannot be carried away. Journal stage The Split Seam.

In old worlds, where the End was explored before the changes, a seam comes apart right next to a player.

Events

  • The Facet, Hive, Breath or Eye Inhales (stage 2, rift open): the wind pulls toward the rift's side, dropped things creep — keep away from the island edge (crouching and a suit hold you). At the end comes the exhale: the split seams of that side spill out the matter of their world.
  • Gathering of the Messengers (stage 2): six endermen carry blocks to a split seam in a ring. Stay to the end and do not look them in the eyes — you get a gift: blocks of the world beyond the rift and Messengers' Nacre. One look — and the whole ring turns on you. Journal stage Gathering of the Messengers.
Spoiler: this describes a place reached late in the game.
  • Sundered Starfall (stage 3): stars fall from the zenith, and small shards strike near players — craters lined with Shard Glass, now and then with a Core Fragment. At its height a large shard 7–10 blocks tall drives into the end stone: a crust of glass around a core and a Sundered Record at its foot; after a day it goes back into the void. Journal stage Sundered Starfall.
  • At stage 3 the silhouettes of the Interrift islands show high in the sky of the End.

The End changes

When the End's stage rises, every player in the End sees the strong moment The End changes: the light shifts to the color of the stage, a pattern of cracks runs over the end stone, an eye with rays opens on the vault of the sky, shards fly, and a line comes over the uplink.

Spoiler: this reveals part of the story and lore.

At stage 4, in the light of the epilogue, the split seams heal with a bright golden seam and the sky of the End warms with a glow along the horizon; the vent mouths of the Nether burn an even gold, and the light of the Nether turns evenly warm.

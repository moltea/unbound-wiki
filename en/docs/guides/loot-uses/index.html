Potions and charges from loot

What to brew and throw from creature parts and guardian and master trophies: guardian drafts, world potions, world charges, master elixirs and charges.

Creature parts and lair guardian trophies are not only for gear: you brew them in a brewing stand and throw them as charges. Everything here is used up, so creatures and guardians are worth hunting again.

Guardian drafts

A guardian trophy dropped into a stand with three Awkward Potions gives three bottles of a draft — a strong potion of two or three effects that cannot be brewed any other way. Gunpowder makes it splash, dragon's breath makes it lingering.

World master trophies

A world master can be called again with an offering, and its second trophy — a heart, a core, a spore, a lens, a lung — no longer piles up for nothing. The first one wakes the gate and goes into the weapon; every next one brews into the master's elixir (three bottles from three Awkward Potions, stronger than any guardian draft) or makes four master charges: the trophy, two gunpowder and a game item. A master charge strikes with the master's own move — many stay where they land and strike again.

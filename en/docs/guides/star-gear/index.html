Zero-G Forge and star gear

A module of your own station where materials of the Solar System, the interstellar places and the ends of the roads become armor, weapons and artifacts.

The zero-g forge is a module of your own station (Module Kit: Zero-G Forge): a glass sphere between four magnetic pillars with the chamber heart (Zero-G Chamber Heart) in the middle. Without weight the melt neither settles nor separates, so here you forge what no planet can cast: star gear made from the materials of the Solar System.

How to forge

  1. Press the forge console — a window opens with the recipe book on the left.
  2. Press a recipe: the materials from your inventory lay themselves into the four cells (the rest goes back). Whatever is missing, the window names in a line.
  3. The “Forge” button starts the chamber. Drops of metal in the recipe’s colors rise in the sphere, circle, merge into the piece, arcs jump between the pillars — and a flash at the end.
  4. The finished piece waits in the output cell. Forging runs on world time: you can leave and come back.

There is one forge per station, and it forges one piece at a time. The forge belongs to the station, like its hold: the window opens only for the station's owner, and a guest is told “This is not your station”.

The Gilded gift. With a Gilded one in the station crew, alloys are 20% cheaper: every count in the recipe ×0.8 (never below one). The window says so in a line.

A sky with no sun in it is not light. The “in the light” and “in the dark” conditions of the Warp Shroud and of the Mycelial Armor (Gatherer's Cap, Bracket-Fungus Coat, Felted Leggings, Rooted Boots) count the sky as light only where there is day and night — on Earth, the planets and the moons. In the Mycelium, the Interrift, the Underweave, the Depths and other worlds without a day cycle only lamps, fire and glowing blocks are light: under the sky of the Mycelium the Mycelial Armor does not wilt, knits wounds and keeps its full armor, and weakens only near bright light (block light above 7).

The Lightbearer needs a blueprint. Its recipe is in everyone’s forge book, but the forge only takes it from someone who has read the Tablet of Hunger in the Swarm Archive; without it the window says “This needs a blueprint: the Tablet of Hunger”.

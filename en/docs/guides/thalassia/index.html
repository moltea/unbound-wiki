Thalassia

A second Earth by another star: the way there, biomes, life, liquids, ores and the Flame Separator, the people.

Thalassia is a moon of the blue giant Pontus by the orange star Hemera. You can breathe there, the water is warm, gravity is 0.9 of Earth's and a day is 20 minutes: no suit or dome is needed and ordinary crops grow. Pontus hangs high with phases, hides Hemera every noon for a few minutes and lights the nights.

The way

  1. Build a radio telescope and record the voice in Leo: it speaks for four minutes around its meridian, once a star day.
  2. Record it again on another star day or from another place (the Moon, an orbit): the shift gives the distance, and the Pontus Mouth appears on the instruments.
  3. Fly there by a starship: 60° ahead of Jupiter, over the swarm of Trojans. Steer right into the middle of the sphere with another sky inside.
  4. Point at the turquoise moon and enter its sky like any world. The way home is the Sol Mouth in the Hemera system.

Land and timber

Eleven biomes: the Thalassian Sea, Turquoise Shallows, Finger Isles, Column Coast, Parasol Forest, Glowing Meadows, Breezy Downs, Tidal Mangroves, Ochre Mesas, Rift Canyons and Polar Tundra. Three woods — parasol (four saplings in a square grow a giant), tidewood and lanternwood — have full sets of blocks and signs; tidestone and column basalt are families of stone. The rock holds the ores of home and aquamarine. The biomes carry the common c: tags, so on NeoForge ores of other mods that choose biomes by tag (Create's zinc) generate there too.

The sea and the liquids

  • Tidewater works like water for farms, creatures and boats, holds you up and glows at night where stirred; a source multiplies, so a pond can stand on Earth too. Its plants live only in it: ribbonweed, shoal grass, night sea pens, tide fans, barrel sponges and sea grapes — a crop for the bottom.
  • Boiling pitch takes the place of lava: burns, holds three times tighter and sets in water.
  • Tide glow in the mangroves gives night vision for 30 seconds.
  • Hemera oil comes from oil seeps — ochre basalt with amber drips: a source every few minutes, the seep cannot be dug out. A bucket burns for 32,000 ticks in a furnace (lava 20,000, a coal block 16,000), runs in fuel pipes and burns longer than lava in other mods' generators.

Five ores and the Flame Separator

Thalassian ores are families. Their raw ore is parted in the Flame Separator Mk1, which burns Hemera oil: you choose in its panel what to get, and it remembers the choice for each family.

Metals of other mods join by their c: tags when those mods are installed. Usually one raw gives one; rare ones take more (emerald, platinum, uranium 3, diamond and iridium 4). Each raw needs an eighth of a bucket of oil and 4,800 ⚡: 10 seconds in Mk1, 1.25 in Mk4. The recipe viewer shows every variant.

The Thalassians

Amphibious farmers of the tide fields; their hamlets stand on glowing meadows and downs above the sea. Trade in emeralds and aquamarine: saplings, barley, stardust, air candles; the weaver sells armor lining, module blanks and multitool modules and mends an item in your hand. The chief and three masters have 23 tasks.

On the shallows stands the Drowned Hamlet, on the column coast the Tide Lighthouse: its Tide Lens sweeps two beams at night.

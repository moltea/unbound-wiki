Rovers

Ground vehicles for other worlds: three chassis, seven modules, charge, repair and the pocket capsule.

A rover carries you, your friends and your things over another world — and folds into one item when you fly on.

Assembly and driving

Put a chassis, up to four modules and a Charged Battery into the Rover Bay (it needs no energy) and press Assemble: the rover stands three blocks in front of it. Keys are those of a boat: throttle, brake and steering; sprint is a quarter faster, jump is the handbrake. In the seat the inventory key opens the panel: the battery slot, the modules, the trunk and your own inventory. A battery is 8,000 ⚡, and without charge the rover does not move.

Fold up in the panel turns the rover with everything in it into a Rover Capsule; use the capsule on level ground and it unfolds.

The worlds

Low gravity sends a rover flying off every bump, Martian dust makes it skid, the acid of Venus wears the tires: mend them with the Tire Repair Kit (half per kit). Wheels burn in lava and crawl in water — take the Ray there.

Hull and repair

Creatures, explosions and lava hurt the hull. Put Hull Plate into the Repair slot of the panel or use one on the rover: each gives back 4 of hull. A wrecked rover drops as a capsule with everything inside and a quarter of its hull.

Together

Only the owner and their /team drive it and open the panel; others ride as passengers. A player who leaves the game keeps the seat.

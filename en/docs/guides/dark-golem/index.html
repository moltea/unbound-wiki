Dark Matter Golem

A trader between the roads made of an iron block and two lightning rods and woken by Dark Grains: how to build it, what it trades and with whom, and its stock of deals.

The Dark Matter Golem is an iron block between two lightning rods, woken by a grain of dark matter. It hangs where it was made, bubbles of its matter circle it, and sparks leap from its copper points. The golem does not fight: it trades the goods of one road for those of another, one for two.

…

Building it

  1. Put a lightning rod on an iron block and another one under it (weathered ones work too).
  2. Click the iron block with Dark Grains; they come from the dark matter cloud of the Main Belt.

The scene fades to violet, two bolts of lightning strike the upper point, a glowing pattern spreads seven blocks over the ground, and the golem turns to the one who built it. The grain is spent (not in creative mode). The first build is the Star Journal stage Dark Matter Golem (after Sky Anomalies); the stage has a blueprint in the journal.

The golem can stand anywhere: on Earth, at your base, on your own station. It drifts back to where it was built if moved, and turns to the nearest player. Only a player can hurt it; broken, it gives back the iron block and the two lightning rods.

Deals

Click the golem and the usual trading window opens. It takes two of one road's goods and gives one of another's:

The golem does not skip progression: it shows a deal only to one who has walked both of its roads, having landed on the world where that fuel is made (the Moon, the Facet, the Forge) or flown in the space where that ore is mined. With no such pair the golem refuses: "you must have walked both yourself"; with some, a line at the bottom of the window reads "Hidden deals: N". So the same golem shows two players different things.

Stock

Each deal has six trades, and the stock is shared by the golem. Each game day brings back two trades to every deal, and Dark Grains used on the golem bring back everything at once (a grain is not spent when the stock is full). The golem and its stock survive a server restart. Jade under the crosshair reminds you: "Trades between the roads", "Dark Grains: restock the deals".

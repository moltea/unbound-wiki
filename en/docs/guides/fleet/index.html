Fleet and dreadnought

Frigates and expeditions of your freighter, and the Drifters' dreadnought.

A freighter can lead up to six frigates. They hold formation beside it and go on expeditions without you — even while you are out of the game.

Frigates

Hire them at a station (the second tab of “Freighters & Frigates”) or ask a saved captain for one instead of the freighter.

Expeditions

Put rocket fuel canisters into the freighter's hold or your inventory, open Fleet in the bridge console, choose one of the four missions of the day and one to four frigates, and send them. The window counts the chance; every missing specialty takes 15%. A mission takes 10–40 minutes and 1–2 canisters per frigate (the empty ones come back); three expeditions may be out at once. Sometimes the crew asks you to decide — answer on the bridge while they are under way, otherwise they play it safe.

The log fills leg by leg. When they return, step onto the decks: the cargo goes into the hold. A damaged frigate is repaired with Hull Plate — 4 per damage for class C, 2 more for each class above; with two damages it waits at home. Only the owner commands the fleet.

The dreadnought

A rare, announced event of open space for owners of a freighter: the first comes no sooner than two hours after you get one, then every four hours, away from stations. You have 45 seconds to fight or leave. The fight lasts through logging out and server restarts.

Spoiler: this describes a boss and its moves.
  1. Break the four shield generators under the columns of light: until then nothing else takes damage, and the shield answers each shot with a blue hexagonal ripple. A hit on a generator shows at once: it flares and throws sparks, and a wounded one smokes and blinks faster the less it has left. Without the shield the armor sheds sparks and scale and glows red-hot where you hit, and a heavy volley sends a shiver of lights along the hull.
  2. Silence the six turrets: keep moving across their line and come from where the hull hides you.
  3. Stay out of the line of the bow: the main gun charges for five seconds and takes 34 of your hull.
  4. Shoot the hangar doors on the port side and fly in (four ships fit), then fight through the holds to the bridge.
  5. Defeat the Drifter Warlord. At half health the breastplate comes off: he charges wall to wall — let the wall stop him.

Then choose. Take the ship at the helm: within 20 minutes restore it with the Command Core, 48 hull plates and 24 tetrataenite ingots — it becomes a class D freighter with the decks, hold and hangar of class B, and it makes the fleet stronger. Or overload the reactor and run: it blows up in 75 seconds and gives half as much loot again. Dropped things and ships come back to you either way.

Void Shoals

The End's own trade: void currents at the island edges, the safety line and its yank, drift finds, the net, anchor plates for hanging platforms and the diver's rudder.

Once a rift is open in the End, pale void currents in the color of that rift rise from the void at the edges of the outer islands on its side of the sky. This is where you dive: jump with a safety line, let the currents carry you, and catch the finds drifting in them. While the End sleeps (stage 0, see “Nether and End changes”) or the changes are switched off, there are no currents and no finds; the main island is left alone — currents rise only by the outer islands.

Currents

  • A current stands at an island edge: a column from the bottom of the world up to the rim, two blocks above the ground. Its axis slowly moves in a circle.
  • Inside a current you are lifted — faster at the axis, slower at the edge; toward the top the lift fades and you hover at the rim while the current carries you to it. Over the ground it lets go — you step onto the island.
  • Between currents you fall. Below 22 blocks above the bottom, the void pulls down harder than normal gravity.
  • With an elytra, a current is an updraft. During Void Pull the currents are a third stronger.
  • The deeper the dive, the richer the finds.

Safety line

The Safety Line is a spool of thread spun from void shards.

WhatHow
Hookuse it on a block in the End — that is the anchor
Unhooksneak and use it in the air
Length64 blocks over the ground from the anchor and 4 turns; on an Anchor Plate — half as long again
Yankfall 6 blocks below the bottom of the world and the line yanks you back to the anchor, one turn of the spool per yank
  • The line does not let you farther than its length over the ground; up and down are free; you cannot hang on it.
  • The last turn — the spool is spent. Beyond 1.25 lengths from the anchor the line cannot reach you, and a broken anchor holds nothing: then the void keeps you. Without a line you get one warning per dive near the bottom.
  • A yank is the strong moment The line holds; others see a sign and hear a sound.
  • Left the game in mid-fall — you come back at your anchor, and no turn is spent.
  • Go farther than 2.5 lengths over the ground and the line unhooks itself. The anchor is kept in the line itself: every player has their own, and it survives leaving and a server restart.
  • The Long Safety Line has 112 blocks and 8 turns.

Drift finds

Drift finds float up in the currents — glowing icons of things in a halo: near a diver every few seconds, no more than four per diver, more often deeper down. After a couple of minutes the current lets a find go and it sinks below the bottom. A find goes to whoever touches it or pulls it in with the net — not necessarily the one it surfaced for.

A Messenger Cache is a small chest the endermen dropped; it opens in hand: void shards, ender pearls, Messengers' Nacre; from deep down also pebbles, the matter of the world, chorus fruit and a shulker shell.

The Diver’s Net pulls the nearest find within ten blocks in front of your eyes to your hands — so you can catch them from the island rim.

Anchor plates

Void pebbles are pressed into an Anchor Plate. In the End a plate holds in the void with no support: use it in the air and it settles three blocks in front of your eyes (look at a block and it is placed like an ordinary block). Hanging platforms over the void are built on plates; from below a plate glows with an anchor sign. In other worlds it is an ordinary block. A line hooked to a plate reaches half as far again.

Diver's Rudder

The Diver’s Rudder is belt gear: it lifts you 1.35 times faster in currents, lets you steer your flight with the movement keys in the air under the islands, and weakens the pull near the bottom. The price: your safe fall height is one block lower.

Giant digs

The Nether's own trade: Giant remains, layered magma under pressure, the heat probe, the digger's chisel, the coolant lance, the Giant's cradle and the everlasting hearth.

The Giants once walked the fire road, and their bones still lie in the rock of the Nether — centuries in magma have fired them like clay. Once a vent is open (stage 1, see “Nether and End changes”), the remains come alive: the rock around the fired bones is shot through with layered magma under pressure. You dig carefully here: open a pocket the wrong way and you get lava in your face and cracked bones. While the Nether sleeps or the changes are switched off, the rock at the remains is cold: neither bone nor magma can be broken — "The bone is held fast by cold rock: while the Nether sleeps, it will not come out."

Remains

A Giant Remains is a mound of rock under arches of broken ribs; a skull peeks out of the slope, and four more bones lie one or two blocks under the face. Remains lie in soul sand valleys, basalt deltas and nether wastes (not in the forests). A vent breach sometimes blows remains out of the slope next to the new mouth — so remains come to old worlds too.

Pressure

Layered Magma has a pressure from 0 to 3. A pocket is a set of connected magma blocks (up to 32). It answers when an empty space appears next to it (air, fire, grass; rock, lava and water do not count):

PressureWhat happens when it is opened
1–2the pocket vents steam — the pressure of the whole pocket drops by a step
3the pocket swells white-hot, hisses and spurts (everyone within 8 blocks is warned); a second and a half — plug the hole, pour coolant or run
3, too latea blowout: lava into the opened cells, fire, 5 damage and knockback to everyone within 4 blocks, bones nearby crack; after 5 seconds the lava goes out

Breaking a full pocket itself blows it out at once. Every hidden bone has a chain 3 → 2 → 1 toward the nearest face: dig from that side, and each venting turns a two into a one and a three into a two. Under the skull lies a three, under the middle of the mound a sealed pocket of threes: only coolant takes it.

Tools

  • The Heat Probe in hand shows the magma within 4 blocks behind the face you look at as glowing spots: dark cherry — 1, orange — 2, trembling whitish yellow — 3, gray-blue — cooled; bones are cold blue spots. Use it on rock for a reading: how many pockets are near, the fullest one, how many bones.
  • The Digger's Chisel — only with it does a bone come out whole (or cracked, if it already cracked); any other tool crumbles it into 1–3 Fired Bone Chip. Take a bone out next to a full three and it comes out cracked, and the pocket blows.
  • A Coolant Canister on an opened pocket cools the whole pocket to zero (the empty canister comes back).
  • The Coolant Lance is set point first into the rock and once a second cools the pocket up to three blocks behind by a step, without opening it. Coolant comes through fuel pipes or from a canister (tank 4000, a canister adds 1000).

Giant's Cradle

The Giant's Cradle is the headstone; 3 by 5 Cradle Stone are laid in front of it. Bones are put as items on the stones of the middle row, each in its own place, from the headstone: skull, Fired Clavicle, Fired Rib, Fired Vertebra, Fired Phalanx. A bone in the wrong place — a line tells you whose place it is.

The cradle can face any of the four sides: it runs away from the headstone the way you look when you place it. Cradle stones turn by themselves so that their gold-lined groove runs along the cradle — whichever way you look when you place them, and even if you lay them before the headstone; a stone away from a cradle lies the way you look. The Giant lies on its back: the skull looks up at the sky with its crown toward the headstone, and the hand at the far end spreads its fingers outward.

A whole skeleton of one Giant brings the strong moment The Giant Remembers: light in the Giant's color, veins across the floor with the vent sign, a shadow of heat over the cradle and a line of the Giant's memory over the uplink. A few seconds later the cradle becomes an everlasting hearth:

  • a Coal Generator Mk1 by the headstone (beside it, above it or two blocks above) burns without fuel and never cools down;
  • within 12 blocks of the cradle the cold of the world does not reach you — of any world (heat stays): a cradle works in the Ash and on the ice giants alike;
  • every 5 seconds the players in its warmth get the Giant's gift:
GiantGift
Vault-BearerResistance

Take the cradle apart and the hearth goes out, the bones come off; build it again and the Giant remembers again.

What to do with the bones

  • Whole bones make gear of the fire road: the Fired Vertebra Charm (fire and lava −35%, an absorption shield every 30 seconds) and the Fired Phalanx Ring (strikes whoever hits you with heat and knocks them back, every 2 seconds).
  • Cracked ones (Cracked Fired Bone) and chips make Fired Bone Meal, Fired Bone Block and Fired Bone Tiles; the meal goes into cradle stones.

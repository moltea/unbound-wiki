Wiki

The mod’s reference and guides: everything in it, with recipes, creatures, worlds and lore.

Unbound is a mod about looking up and going where you look. The telescope finds worlds in the sky, the rocket flies to the planets, the riftscope opens the void beyond the End and the magmascope the fire beneath the Nether. Further on: base machines and the spacesuit, peoples and their tasks, dungeons with the masters of the worlds, ships and open space.

The Sky Road

The telescope, constellations, rockets and the planets of the Solar System — then ships and interstellar space.

The Dark Road

The riftscope in the End, rifts and the four worlds beyond them — the shuttle, the Interrift and its secrets.

The Fire Road

The magmascope in the Nether, vents and the worlds at their bottom — the bell, the Depths and the Nether Sun.

How to use the wiki

  • Getting started — first steps: the journal, a lens, a telescope and the first night.
  • The roads — where each of the three roads leads, without unnecessary detail.
  • Guides — how the instruments and mechanics work, hands-on: what to press and where to look.
  • Reference — every item, block, creature, world, people and ship has its own page: where to get it, recipes, what it is used for, numbers and lore. The reference is built from the mod’s own files, so it always matches the game.
  • Search — Ctrl K (or ⌘ K): pages, items, creatures, worlds and tasks.

Spoilers

Bosses and their moves, late places and part of the lore are hidden under a blur — reveal them one by one or all at once with the “Show spoilers” switch in the header. The mod’s central mystery is not revealed here at all: players find it out themselves.

The path in brief

  1. Getting started — the journal, a lens and the first telescope.
  2. The sky road — the telescope finds worlds, rockets fly to them, the spacesuit lets you breathe there, and machines power the base.
  3. On the worlds — peoples and their tasks, dungeons and world masters, gates.
  4. The other two roads — the riftscope in the End and the magmascope in the Nether.
  5. Instruments — the spectral laboratory and the radio telescope.
  6. Space — ships, the peoples’ stations, your own station with its observatory and zero-g forge.
  7. The ends of the roads — the later guides, each behind spoilers; every guide ends with a “What next” section that leads on.

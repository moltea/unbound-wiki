Advancements

Five tabs of advancements: conditions and rewards.

The mod's advancements live in the game's advancement window (the L key): 150 of them in five tabs — The Path, Worlds, Life, Tech and Challenges. The star journal leads the way; advancements mark events and challenges. Whatever you did before the update counts at your next login.

Challenges

Master challenges count for every fighter separately. No damage — from the moment you join the fight to the victory nothing hurt you: not the master, not a fall, not lava (a shield, dodging and armor are fair). No armor — the armor slots are empty both when you join and when you win; the spacesuit does not count. Three minutes run from the moment you join. A fight begun before a server restart gives no challenges, and neither do guardians of lairs. A fight everyone left starts anew.

Compatibility

Which mods the mod works well with: recipe viewers, tooltips, Sodium and Iris, energy and tags.

Recipe viewers — JEI and REI

All of the mod’s recipes are visible in the viewer: the crafting table and furnaces, the mod’s machines (crusher, melt pump, zero-g forge, cryo-distiller, shipyard…), altar offerings and gate awakening. Items without a recipe have a “where to get it” page: loot, creatures, caches, trades, tasks. Items that come in variants — ship blueprints, Ringwright tablets, Orphan Records, copper patina stages — are listed in the viewer one by one. JEI and REI work on both loaders.

“What am I looking at” — Jade

Jade shows what is inside a machine or pipe, whether it is running and which upgrades it has, whether a guardian is asleep, who the natives are and whether they have a task for you.

Energy and fluids of other mods

A converter links the mod’s network to the energy of other mods (Tech Reborn, AE2, Powah): on Fabric via Team Reborn Energy, on NeoForge via the loader’s shared energy (FE). The mod’s fluids flow through other mods’ pipes and tanks on both loaders. On NeoForge, Pipez pipes take energy straight from the mod’s generators and batteries (pipe side set to extract) and fuel from its tanks. Buckets of the mod’s fluids empty into other mods’ tanks and fill from them on both loaders.

Hoppers and other mods’ item pipes put things into the input slots of the mod’s machines and take out what is ready, the same on both loaders: modules, upgrades and rocket cargo are never pulled out by a pipe, even one that names no side, and a slot takes no more than the machine’s window allows (16 empty canisters in the Fuel Plant’s canister slot).

Common tags

The mod’s things are in the common c: tags — that is how other mods’ recipes, machines and sorters recognize them:

  • ores (c:ores), raw materials (c:raw_materials), ingots (c:ingots), dusts (c:dusts), gems (c:gems), storage blocks (c:storage_blocks), the copper sheet and gear (c:plates, c:gears);
  • fluid buckets (c:buckets), the stained glass of the worlds and other glass (c:glass_blocks, c:glass_panes);
  • food and drink (c:foods — raw and cooked meat, soups, berries; c:drinks), armor (c:armors) and weapons (c:tools);
  • world masters and lair guardians are in the entity tag c:bosses: other mods’ traps and teleporters leave them alone.

Every item tag of the mod has a name in both languages: a recipe viewer shows "Titanium Ingots" or "Lead Ores" instead of #c:ingots/titanium. Common tags that the loader names itself (c:ingots, c:ores) keep the loader’s names.

The mod’s crusher processes other mods’ raw materials by the same tags, and the recipes of the crusher and the zero-g forge are ordinary data pack recipes: any pack or mod can add its own.

Sodium, Iris and shader packs

The mod works with Sodium and Iris. With a shader pack on, the mod’s post effects (climate blur, flight effects) switch off by themselves and the telescope sky is drawn over the frame — everything else works as usual. In Sodium’s video settings the mod has its own “Mod graphics” page (the level and a “Mod settings…” button) — on Fabric and on NeoForge.

Tested with the Complementary Reimagined pack on Fabric and on NeoForge (Iris for NeoForge; Oculus and Embeddium have no builds for this game version). In airless worlds and in space (the Moon, Mercury, Pluto, space, the Depths) the pack is deliberately not applied, so the mod’s sky looks the same as without it. In other worlds the pack draws its own Earth-like sky and clouds. The pack does not read the fog inside gas giant clouds, so the mod lays it itself: inside a cloud everything beyond seven blocks fades into the cloud’s color, as without a pack, and the glint of cloud ore shows on top of the haze.

Conveniences

  • Trinkets Updated — accessory slots where the worlds’ artifacts and the knapsack are worn (Fabric and NeoForge).
  • Curios (NeoForge) — the same: ring, necklace, belt, back (knapsack, the Visitor’s Sail), head (the Ringwright Lens); with both slot mods installed, the slots of both work. You need Curios 17.0.0-beta.3 or newer: earlier betas for 26.3 do not start on the current NeoForge. The Curios window opens with G — the same key as gear details: holding G over an artifact opens its details, and G away from an item closes the window. Tooltips name the slot mod you have; without one an artifact cannot be put on, and the knapsack works right from the inventory.
  • Controls on NeoForge. NeoForge 26.3 (beta) marks many of the game’s own keys yellow — Strafe Left, Open Chat, the number keys: they share a letter with the F3 debug combos, and the loader counts that as a conflict. The mod’s keys are not marked there — they are judged the way the game itself and Fabric do it: gear details on G and the telescope hint on H get in the way of neither F3+G and F3+H nor Quick Actions; a mark appears only if you rebind a key onto a taken one.
  • Mod Menu — mod settings from the mod list on Fabric; on NeoForge the same button is in its own mod list, no extra mod needed.
  • AppleSkin — saturation of the mod’s food.
  • Maps (Xaero’s, JourneyMap) and sorting (Inventory Profiles Next, Mouse Tweaks) work with the mod’s screens.

Ship Module: Interstellar Drive I

Steps

  1. Craft the drive: its heart is the Threshold Key, given by the ring at the edge of Pluto; around it are heart ice, osmiridium, tetrataenite and two Hawking sparks from Planet Nine.
  2. Put it into a free module socket of a class A or better starship: ship console (E).
  3. Record where to go: with an Imaging Plate in your inventory aim a star telescope at the Pleiades, behind the back of Taurus, and press Left-click. The Orion Nebula needs an astrograph.
  4. In the cockpit open the map “Interstellar” (M), choose the place and press “PLOT THE COURSE”. Look at the line of the way back first.
  5. Turn the nose onto the course mark in the sky and hold ` for three seconds.
  6. Press , in the place: dust hides much from the eye. To go home, put the nose on the home mark and hold ` again.

Keys

  • M — the map “Interstellar”

Conditions and limits

  • A jump takes about six buckets of fuel and 16 stardust, more the farther (Pleiades 5.8 for tier I). Then the drive cools for two minutes.
  • Reach: “Threshold” 1,500, “Pulsar” 8,000, “Quasar” 30,000 light-years; the last never costs more than 5.8 buckets.
  • Tier II: Threshold drive, pulsar navigator, two star seeds of Orion, ring spark. Tier III: pulsar drive, quasar reference, ring spark, four neutron dust (Crab), two disk plasma (Cygnus X-1).
  • Only a starship of the planets’ road carries the drive. Emergency beacon home from the map: no fuel, 32 stardust; without dust the ship arrives with a quarter of its hull.

If it does not work

  • “No interstellar drive in the module slots” — put the module into a socket; a road ship of the rift or vent cannot.
  • “Navigation record missing” — record that place: telescope, radio telescope, tablet or anomaly data.
  • The map warns the way back will not suffice — take canisters; charge again to go anyway.

Manual

How to use everything in the mod: steps, keys, conditions.

How to use the machines, instruments, vehicles and places of the mod: numbered steps, keys, conditions and limits, and what to do when it does not work. These are the same pages as in the game: the Manual tab of the star journal, or hold G over an item in any window. Keys are named by their defaults — in the game the pages show your own.

First steps

  • Observatory building set — Decorative blocks for a telescope house.
  • Star Journal — The journal keeps your path: what to do next, how it is done, and everything you have found.
  • Polished Lens — Optical parts: every telescope and many instruments start with a polished lens.
  • Refractor Telescope — The first telescope: the Moon, Mars and the brightest constellations.
  • Looking through a telescope — The sky itself changes while you look: turn your head, join stars into constellations and focus on planets.

Instruments and labs

  • Reflector Telescope — The second telescope: zooms 3 times and shows the planets up to Jupiter.
  • Astrograph and graviscope — Two telescopes too heavy for a tripod: they stand on a base of blocks.
  • Magmascope — Listens to the magma under the Nether and finds vents — roads to four worlds.
  • Reading a spectrum — Burns a sample and shows the lines of its substances; what you know opens recipes and instruments.
  • Spectrograph: light of the Sun and worlds — Catches the light of the sky for the same puzzle as in the laboratory: what is the air there made of.
  • Ore Scanner — Shows veins of ores whose standard you have recorded in the laboratory.
  • Unknown samples and weak spots — Finds that open in the laboratory, and parts of creatures that tell how to hit them harder.
  • Building a radio telescope — Hears what no eye sees, by day and in rain, on Earth, on the Moon and in open space.
  • Radio signals: four instruments — Every kind of signal is tuned with its own instrument, and the line under the map hints which.
  • Rare signals: series and Pontus — Two signals that come by the clock: the way to a magnetar and to Thalassia.
  • Interferometer of three roads — Three radio telescopes on three roads make one night’s image of the Galactic Center.
  • Riftscope — Looks into the void of the End and finds rifts — the ways to four worlds.

Energy and machines

  • First power grid — From the first generator to a machine that runs: touching blocks and beams of light.
  • Coal Generator — Burns anything a furnace burns and turns it into energy.
  • Machine sides and redstone — Where hoppers, pipes and energy enter and leave a machine, and how to switch it off.
  • Solar Panel — Energy from daylight, without fuel.
  • Battery block — Stores spare energy and gives it back; charges portable batteries for rockets.
  • Induction Emitter — Powers every machine in a ball around it without beams.
  • Entangled Resonator — Carries energy between two bases at any distance, even between worlds.
  • Energy converter and other mods — A bridge between the beam grid and cables and machines of other mods.
  • Gravity Generator — Sets the gravity you choose in a ball around it.
  • Machine versions and cards — A higher version works faster and holds more; a card changes one thing.
  • Multimeter and manometer — Show what is going on in the beam grid and in the pipes.
  • Crusher — Two dusts from one raw ore; glass, salt and fuel from the rock of other worlds.
  • Melt Pump — Draws metal and crystals out of molten seas and lakes.
  • Flame Separator — Burns Hemera oil to turn raw Thalassian ore into the ore of home you choose.
  • Fuel plant — Rocket fuel from coal and helium-3 from regolith. It needs no energy.
  • Charging station and batteries — Charges portable batteries and the multitool from the sun.
  • Fuel Depot — Stores one liquid fuel and shares it through fuel pipes.
  • Liquids and buckets — Where each liquid comes from, how to take it and what to do with it.
  • Pearl Liquefier — Melts ender pearls into liquid pearl, the fuel of the rift shuttle.
  • Cryo Still — Turns ice and magma cream into coolant, the fuel of the diving bell.
  • How machines work — What every machine has in common: energy, sides, hoppers, redstone, upgrades.
  • Electric Furnace — Smelts everything a furnace smelts, twice as fast and without coal.

Air, suit and climate

  • Bioreactor — Turns plants into oxygen: the first source of air for a base and a suit.
  • Electrolyzer — Splits water into oxygen and rocket fuel hydrogen, and draws oxygen out of the ground where there is no ice.
  • Sealed room and airlock — A room full of air on any world: breathe without a suit, with no heat, cold or acid.
  • Oxygen Dome — Holds a ball of air without walls: breathe, plant saplings, keep a respawn bed.
  • Acid Scrubber — Keeps the air of Venus and the Breath from eating you around a base. It gives no oxygen.
  • Hydroponic Bed — Grows crops without soil, water, air or light — and closes the circle of air on a base.
  • Suit Rack — Sets modules into the torso of the suit and fills it with oxygen and charge.
  • Suit modules: which for which world — Four sockets of the torso decide how long and where you live.
  • Spacesuit and oxygen — Without air and a whole suit you live seconds in another world.

Storage and the base

  • Storage: terminal and cells — All your things in one window with search and sorting.
  • Crafting from the storage — A crafting grid in the storage window: parts come from the storage.
  • Pneumatic mail — Carries things between chests and machines in capsules through tubes.
  • Auto-assembler — Makes one chosen thing again and again by a recipe card.
  • Interworld channel — Links the storages of two worlds: you see the far one’s things and take them.
  • Lift — A cabin that carries you and others between floors.
  • Jump beacon — Jumps you between your bases inside one world.
  • Courier drone — A drone carries things between two storages where no tube reaches.
  • Knapsack — A worn store that also keeps the suit supplied.

Rockets and vehicles

  • Launch Gantry — Fills a rocket, shuttle or bell by hose, straight from fuel pipes.
  • Charging Gantry — Charges the batteries of a rocket on its table straight from the grid.
  • Star map and a flight — From the cockpit of a rocket you choose a world on the star map and launch.
  • Building a rocket — A rocket is built block by block on a launch table; each next one flies farther.
  • Rocket modules — Modules go into sockets on the hull and decide whether you can come back.
  • Rift Shuttle — Goes from the End through open rifts into four worlds and comes home by its thread for free.
  • Diving Bell — Dives from the Nether through lava to four worlds at the bottom of the vents; the winch hauls it home for free.
  • Rovers — A ground vehicle for other worlds: seats, cargo, air around it; folds into a pocket capsule.

Starships and freighters

  • Flying a ship — Take off, leave the sky for open space, land — and what each key of the cockpit does.
  • Jump and system map — Crosses thousands of blocks in four seconds.
  • Shipyard: building a ship — The shipyard builds ships, raises their class and takes them apart.
  • Ship console — One window for the hold, modules, fuel, repair, friends and live cargo of a ship.
  • Friends and crew of a ship — Lets other players aboard, to the helm or to the console — even while you are offline.
  • Ship Beacon — Calls your ship to you from wherever it stands.
  • Ship modules — Modules make a ship faster, tougher and roomier; each kind has three tiers.
  • Raising a ship’s class — C → B → A → S: every class makes the same ship better in everything.
  • The deck of a ship — Every ship has an interior you can walk while it flies.
  • Autopilot — Flies the ship through space by itself while you walk the deck.
  • System map — A view from above on the space you are in: worlds, belts, stations and your finds.
  • Landing Beacon — A ship from space enters the sky right above it — no more landing thousands of blocks from home.
  • Live cargo — Carries animals, villagers and other creatures between worlds aboard your ship.
  • Crossing from ship to ship — A sleeve of light carries you to another ship in space or on the ground.
  • Salvage: a ship in the hold — The grapple folds an empty ship into your hold. No module is needed.
  • Tow — A rope of light drags a ship with people aboard, or one that does not fit your hold.
  • Belt ores and the mining beam — Ores that no world has hang in space: mine them with a pickaxe or from the cockpit.
  • Wrecked ships — Remains of old expeditions: a chest, a story, and sometimes a ship you can raise.
  • Ship weapons — The pulse cannon: a beam that hits where you aim at once.
  • Heavy weapons and shield — A piercing slug, homing torpedoes and a shield that takes hits before the hull.
  • Weapons of the roads — The void lance, the slag flak, and flares against torpedoes.
  • Freighter: docking, key, jumps — A capital ship that carries your ships, your stores and your base, and comes when called.
  • Freighter decks — Inside: a hangar, a build hall, a hold in one storage network, crew rooms and a bridge with a view of space.
  • Buying and exchanging a freighter — Every station of a people sells three freighters a day and takes yours in exchange.
  • Distress call — Save a freighter from the Drifters — the first one saved becomes yours.
  • Fleet and expeditions — Frigates follow your freighter and go on expeditions by themselves, even while you are offline.
  • Dreadnought

Worlds, peoples and dungeons

  • Village crafts — Villagers of five new trades; their work blocks are machines of this mod.
  • Natives: talking and trading — Each of the worlds has its own people: they talk, trade, sell maps and keep tasks for you.
  • Native tasks — Natives give tasks: bring, defeat, find, survive, deliver — and pay with things nobody else sells.
  • Finding structures of the worlds — Structures of a world hide loot of that world and pages for the journal about the place.
  • Dungeons: in, down and out — A dungeon of a world is several floors with a locked gate on each, a guard, and the master with a treasury at the bottom.
  • Dungeons: seals and locks — What the seals in dungeons and ruins wait for and how each lock works.
  • Dungeons: what each one is about — Every big dungeon has one mechanism of its own on all its floors; a change is seen and heard 3–6 seconds ahead.
  • Masters of worlds: how to summon
  • Guardians of lairs
  • World gates — Awakened gates are linked: from any of them you step to any other, for a small toll.
  • World events — Every few days something big happens in a world; who stays there to the end has survived it.
  • Weather of the worlds — Weather comes and goes by itself and slightly changes the world: what you breathe, see, and how you move.
  • Ores of the worlds — Each world has two or three ores of its own in its own rock; they pay for almost everything the worlds give.
  • Food and plants of the worlds — Plants and beasts of a world give food that helps to live in it: warmth, coolness, acid resistance, air.
  • Breeding beasts of worlds — Some beasts eat from your hand, and a fed pair has young — only under the sky of their own world.
  • Taming and riding — Feed a creature that can be tamed until it trusts you; in a harness of its world it carries you on land, in the air or water.
  • Atlas of Life — The Atlas tab of the journal holds a card for every creature: where it lives, what it eats and how to keep it.
  • Meteorites: find and dig — Once in a few days a meteorite falls in wild country, far from homes; it leaves a crater, metal and stardust.
  • Sky Heart — A giant that is not a stone: under the open sky it gathers stardust by night.
  • The way to Thalassia — Thalassia is a second Earth by the star Hemera; the way there is a mouth by Jupiter’s Trojans; the first starship will do.
  • Thalassians and their fields — Thalassians live in hamlets on the meadows above the shallows and trade what keeps a second Earth useful.
  • Garden under the tide — Six plants of Thalassia’s sea live in tidewater; sea grapes are planted in any pond of it.
  • Hemera oil and the liquids of Thalassia — Hemera oil seeps out of ochre basalt and is the best natural fuel: a bucket burns longer than lava.
  • The Under-Ice Whale
  • The Wanderer and its Orphans
  • Leap from Verona — The cliff of Verona on Miranda is 270 blocks high; a snowdrift below takes a jump without harm.
  • Reading the Ringwrights — Signs on stones by the gates are words: you match pictures to them until the phrase makes sense.

Gear and multitool

  • Lightbearer — A blade of light against Shades of Hunger; weak against everything else.
  • Multitool — One charged tool instead of picks and shovels: it mines, cuts, scans and shoots plasma.
  • Module Bench and armor modules — The Module Bench sets modules into sockets of the worlds’ armor and the multitool; they stay when you take the item back.
  • Weapons of the worlds — Weapons of the worlds have an ability and grow with you: every kill raises their mastery.
  • Armor of the worlds — Armor of the worlds is worn in the usual slots over the spacesuit, has sockets and a set bonus.
  • Artifacts — An artifact is a ring, pendant or belt that works while it is worn in a slot of Trinkets or Curios.
  • Radiation on Io — Under the open sky of Io a dose of radiation builds up; the Radiation Screen of the suit stops it entirely.
  • Wings of Titan — In the thick air of Titan a person flies on muscles alone; elsewhere the wings stay folded.
